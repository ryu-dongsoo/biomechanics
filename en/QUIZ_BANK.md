This document contains weekly quiz questions and advanced engineering discussion topics to deepen your understanding of biomechanics and dynamics.

## 📌 Weekly Quizzes & Discussions

### Week 01: Rectilinear Motion
- **Key Question**: When acceleration is given as a function of velocity $a = f(v)$ or position $a = f(s)$, why is the differential chain-rule relation $a\,ds = v\,dv$ used instead of direct time integration?
- **Discussion Topic**: Designing deceleration profiles for hydraulic cylinder end-of-stroke cushioning in agricultural machinery.

### Week 02: Projectile Motion
- **Key Question**: How does the launch angle for maximum range on an inclined plane differ from that on a flat horizontal surface ($45^\circ$)?
- **Discussion Topic**: Droplet trajectory and aerodynamic drag effects in orchard speed sprayers.

### Week 03: n-t / r-θ Coordinates
- **Key Question**: Why is acceleration non-zero even at constant speed ($\dot{v}=0$) along a curved path, and what is the physical mechanism behind the Coriolis acceleration ($2\dot{r}\dot{\theta}$) in polar coordinates?
- **Discussion Topic**: Particle acceleration analysis on rotary sorting tables and centrifugal fertilizer spreader blades.

### Week 04: Relative & Constrained Motion
- **Key Question**: What are the datum selection rules and sign conventions when differentiating cable length conservation equations in multi-pulley systems?
- **Discussion Topic**: Relative velocity and constrained kinematics of a tractor three-point hitch linkage.

### Week 05: F=ma in Rectangular Coordinates
- **Key Question**: When an external force is applied to a body at rest, why must the maximum static friction force ($F_{s,\max} = \mu_s N$) be checked before applying the kinetic friction coefficient ($\mu_k$)?
- **Discussion Topic**: Tipping and slipping thresholds for agricultural transport vehicles on sloped terrain.

### Week 06: Kinetics of Curvilinear Motion
- **Key Question**: How does the normal contact force $N$ vary between a convex crest and a concave dip on a vertical circular track, and what determines the condition for surface separation ($N=0$)?
- **Discussion Topic**: Critical conveying speed to prevent fruit detachment over curved roller conveyor transitions.

### Week 07: Work and Energy (Work-Energy Principle — Ch.3.6)
- **Key Question**: Compared with Newton's second law ($\Sigma\mathbf{F} = m\mathbf{a}$), what is the primary analytical advantage of the work-energy principle ($T_1 + U_{1-2} = T_2$) when relating velocity to displacement, and why does the normal guide force ($N$) do zero work?
- **Discussion Topic**: Frictional energy dissipation of produce crates sliding down inclined chutes and buffer spring stiffness ($k$) sizing (linked to Problems 3/84 and 3/101).

### Week 09: Potential Energy & Mechanical Energy Conservation (Ch.3.7)
- **Key Question**:
  1. Why can the datum for gravitational potential energy ($V_g = mgh$) be chosen arbitrarily, whereas the reference point for elastic potential energy ($V_e = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$) must always be fixed at the unstretched length ($x=0$)?
  2. In the modified work-energy equation ($T_1 + V_1 + U'_{1-2} = T_2 + V_2$), why must gravitational and spring work never be included again inside the non-conservative work term $U'_{1-2}$?
- **Discussion Topic**: Optimal design of equilibrium angles ($\frac{dV}{d\theta} = 0$) where maximum angular velocity occurs in torsion-spring and counterweight assist mechanisms (linked to Sample Problem 3.18 and Problems 3/111–3/122).

### Week 10: Linear and Angular Impulse & Momentum (Ch.3.8~3.10)
- **Key Question**: During the brief instant when a bullet penetrates a wooden block or two bodies undergo inelastic impact, why is linear momentum conserved ($\Sigma\mathbf{G}_1 = \Sigma\mathbf{G}_2$) while mechanical energy ($T_1 \neq T_2$) is not?
- **Discussion Topic**: Extending contact duration ($\Delta t$) via cushioning pads to minimize peak impact force ($\int F\,dt$) in postharvest fruit sorting lines.

### Week 11: Kinetics of Systems of Particles I (Ch.4.1~4.4)
- **Key Question**: Why do internal forces between particles in a system always sum to zero ($\Sigma\mathbf{f}_i = \mathbf{0}$), and why is the mass-center acceleration $\bar{\mathbf{a}}$ independent of internal connection rigidity (rigid frame vs. pinned bars)?
- **Discussion Topic**: Mass-center dynamics and base reaction force analysis of multi-DOF harvesting robotic arms.

### Week 12: Kinetics of Systems of Particles II: Conservation Laws (Ch.4.5)
- **Key Question**: What is the physical meaning of each of the two terms in the total kinetic energy expression $T = \frac{1}{2}m\bar{v}^2 + \Sigma\frac{1}{2}m_i|\dot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}_i|^2$ for a system of particles?
- **Discussion Topic**: Recoil displacement and velocity analysis of a mobile platform caused by manipulator movement on frictionless rails.

### Week 13: Kinetics of Systems of Particles III: Steady Mass Flow (Ch.4.6)
- **Key Question**: When applying the steady mass flow equation $\Sigma\mathbf{F} = m'(\mathbf{v}_2 - \mathbf{v}_1)$ to a moving vane, why must the mass flow rate $m'$ and relative velocity deflection be evaluated using the relative speed ($v - u$)?
- **Discussion Topic**: Reaction forces and support moments in combine grain unloading augers and high-pressure agricultural sprayer nozzles.

### Week 14: Kinetics of Systems of Particles IV: Variable Mass & Review (Ch.4.7~4.8)
- **Key Question**: When lifting a chain from rest on a floor at constant speed $v$, what is the energetic reason behind the factor-of-two difference ($\rho v^2$ vs. $\frac{1}{2}\rho v^2$) between an open-link chain (Sample Problem 4.10) and an inextensible flexible rope (Sample Problem 4.11)?
- **Discussion Topic**: Traction dynamics under continuous mass loss ($\dot{m} < 0$) in water-spraying trucks and granular fertilizer spreaders.
